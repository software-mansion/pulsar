## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `HapticLottie` widget and `HapticLottieController` to play Pulsar haptics in
  sync with a Lottie animation.
- `realtime` (progress-driven `RealtimeComposer`) and `pattern` (aligned-start
  `PatternComposer`) engine modes.

## 0.1.1

- Accept `pulsar_haptics` `0.2.x`, so apps on Flutter 3.44+ can pick up its built-in
  Kotlin Android build alongside this package. `0.1.x` is still accepted.
