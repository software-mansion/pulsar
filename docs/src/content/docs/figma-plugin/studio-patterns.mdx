The 151 designed presets cover most interactions. When none of them is right,
draw your own in [Pulsar Studio](/pulsar/studio/overview/) — it appears in the
plugin straight away.

**Pulsar Studio Patterns**, at the top of the Presets tab, lists every pattern
on your Pulsar account, grouped by Studio project.

## Creating one

**+ Create new preset** opens Studio in your browser on a blank pattern. Draw
the intensity and frequency envelopes, drop in discrete taps, feel it on a real
device, and save — [The editor](/pulsar/studio/editor/) covers the drawing
itself. The pattern lands in your account's **Figma Plugin** project.

Back in the plugin, expanding the panel re-fetches the list, so a pattern you
just saved is one click away. The **⟳** button forces a refresh.

## Editing one

The **✎** button on any of your patterns reopens it in Studio at the right
project and pattern. Save there and the plugin picks up the new version on its
next refresh — components already bound to it keep their binding and start
playing the updated pattern.

## Binding them

Identical to the library: select a layer, press **+ Add**. Custom patterns work
everywhere a built-in preset does — edit-mode playback, the paired phone, the
shared preview, and the SDK snippet in the details dialog.

The one difference is on the developer's side. A built-in preset is called by
name; a custom pattern is sent as data, and its snippet composes the pattern
rather than naming it. Both are in the details dialog under **Usage**.
