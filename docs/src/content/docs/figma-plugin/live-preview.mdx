Audio in a plugin window tells you the rhythm of a haptic. Only a phone tells
you how it _feels_. The **Live preview** tab pairs one, opens your prototype on
it, and plays the bound haptic whenever you tap an element — while you keep
editing in Figma.

## Step 1 — paste the file link

Figma doesn't hand a plugin the key of the file it is running in, so you paste
it once. In Figma's top bar open **Share → Copy link** and drop it in; a full
link or the bare key both work. It's saved with the file.

<Aside type="caution" title="Set link access to “Anyone with the link”">
  The preview opens your prototype *through* Figma, and Figma can't sign a viewer in. A restricted
  file shows a login screen instead of your design — on your own phone as much as on anyone else's.
</Aside>

<Clip name="figma_link" caption="Where to copy the file link from." />

## Step 2 — pair your phone

Scan the QR with the **Pulsar** app ([App Store][ios] · [Google Play][android])
or type the pairing code. The panel also carries store QR codes if you don't
have the app yet.

actually reachable. The link survives leaving the tab and closing the panel; it
ends when you press **Disconnect** or close the plugin.

## What you get

<Steps>

1. **Tap anything bound, feel it.** Your prototype runs on the phone; tapping an
   element with a haptic on it fires the real vibration.

2. **Play from the plugin.** Pressing **▷** on any preset in the Presets tab
   buzzes the paired phone too — choosing by feel instead of by name.

   <Clip name="play_on_device" caption="Auditioning presets on the paired device." />

3. **The phone follows you.** Select a different top-level frame in Figma and
   the phone jumps to that screen.

4. **Edits arrive on their own.** Bind, rebind or unbind and the change reaches
   the phone within a couple of seconds — no reload, no re-scan.

</Steps>

## The sync status

The pill at the foot of the tab says whether the shared preview matches your
current design.

| Status                 | Meaning                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Not shared yet**     | Nothing has been published for this file              |
| **Syncing…**           | A change is on its way to the server                  |
| **Synced with server** | The preview matches your design                       |
| **Unsynced changes**   | Edits are pending; the refresh button pushes them now |
| **Sync failed**        | It will retry on its own                              |

## If the phone won't connect

- Check that the pasted link is a real Figma link and that the file is shared as
  **Anyone with the link**.
- Make sure the Pulsar app is up to date and the phone has a working connection.
- Re-scan the QR if pairing timed out, or press **Refresh** on the phone card.
