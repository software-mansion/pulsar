## Your subscription

The **Account** tab shows which account you're signed in as and the state of
your plugin licence — a running free trial, an active subscription and when it
renews, or a cancelled one and the date access ends.

<Screenshot
  src={account}
  alt="The Account tab showing an active Bundle subscription renewing in April"
  caption="Manage plan opens Pulsar Studio in your browser; Refresh re-reads the licence after you change it there."
  width={420}
/>

Subscriptions are managed on the web, not in the plugin. Change something in
Studio, come back and press **Refresh**.

## Help & how-to

Below the licence card: **Take the tour** replays the five-step walkthrough, and
a FAQ answers the questions the tour raises — how to bind, how to pair, what
the tags mean, what Pulsar writes into your file, and why a phone might not
connect.

<Screenshot
  src={accountHelp}
  alt="The Help and how-to section with the tour button and the FAQ list"
  caption="The FAQ and the tour share the same text, so they can't drift apart."
  width={420}
/>

**Resources** links out to an onboarding Figma file, an example app to try the
live preview against, and the privacy policy and terms. **Send feedback**
reaches the Pulsar team directly.

## Privacy

<Screenshot
  src={accountPrivacy}
  alt="The two privacy switches: crash reports and Dev Mode annotations"
  caption="Both are on by default and both can be turned off here."
  width={420}
/>

- **Send crash reports** — shares errors and performance data to help fix bugs.
  It never includes your design content.
- **Annotate screens for developers** — marks each screen that has haptics with
  a Dev Mode annotation so developers and their coding tools can see them.
  Turning it off removes the annotations already added.

## Resizing the window

Drag the grip in the bottom-right corner to resize in both directions, or the
right and bottom edges to change just the width or the height. Figma plugin
windows can't be dragged from their outer edges, so these handles are the only
way — and the size is remembered for next time.
